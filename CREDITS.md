## If this becomes a real product

Share-alike is the thing to watch. CC BY-SA obliges derivative works to carry the same licence,
which is awkward once photos sit inside a product. Three ways out, cheapest first:

1. Filter to CC0 and CC BY only, dropping the SA images.
2. License a stock set, or use a provider API (Unsplash, Pexels) with attribution built into the UI.
3. Let people upload their own photo per visit, which is the better product anyway — your
   photograph of a city is more interesting than the canonical one.
